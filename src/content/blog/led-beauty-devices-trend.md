LED therapy masks and devices are the fastest-growing beauty tech category. Learn what to stock for maximum ROI.

LED light therapy has moved from exclusive spa treatments to at-home consumer devices, creating massive wholesale opportunities.

### Multi-Color LED Technology

Modern LED masks offer 7 color wavelengths: red (anti-aging), blue (acne treatment), green (brightening), yellow (anti-redness), purple (firming), cyan (soothing), and white (skin repair).

### What Buyers Look For

High LED bulb count (150+), multiple intensity levels, USB rechargeability, and ergonomic design are key selling points for end consumers.
