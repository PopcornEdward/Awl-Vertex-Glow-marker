Stay ahead of the market with the hottest nail art products and tools that consumers are demanding in 2026.

The nail art industry continues to grow rapidly. As an importer or distributor, stocking the right trending products is crucial for success.

### 1. Chrome & Mirror Effect Powders

Chrome nails remain one of the most requested styles. Stock a full range of chrome powders in silver, gold, rose gold, and holographic finishes.

### 2. Builder Gel & BIAB

Builder-in-a-bottle (BIAB) products are surging as consumers prioritize nail health and strength alongside aesthetics.

### 3. Portable UV/LED Lamps

Compact, USB-rechargeable nail lamps are perfect for the growing mobile nail technician market.
