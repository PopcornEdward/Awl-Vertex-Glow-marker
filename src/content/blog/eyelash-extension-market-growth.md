The global eyelash extension market is projected to reach $2.5 billion by 2030. Here's how importers can capitalize.

The eyelash extension industry shows consistent year-over-year growth driven by social media influence and increasing consumer spending on beauty services.

### Key Product Categories

Individual lashes, premade fans, lash adhesives, application tools, and storage solutions are the five core categories every importer should cover.

### Quality Matters

Korean PBT fiber is the industry gold standard for lash extensions. Ensure your supplier uses certified materials and provides material composition documentation.
