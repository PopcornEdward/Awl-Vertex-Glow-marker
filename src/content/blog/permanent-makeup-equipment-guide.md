Essential PMU machines, pigments, and accessories that every beauty salon supply distributor should know about.

Permanent makeup (PMU) is one of the fastest-growing segments in the beauty industry. From microblading to lip blushing, demand is surging globally.

### Wireless vs. Wired Machines

Wireless PMU pens now dominate the market with their portability and ease of use. Look for brushless motors with adjustable speed and needle depth.

### Pigment Quality

High-quality PMU pigments should be CE and MSDS certified, vegan, and free from heavy metals. Stock a comprehensive color range for brows, lips, and eyeliner applications.
