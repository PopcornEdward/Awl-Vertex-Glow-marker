A complete guide for importers and distributors on sourcing beauty tools directly from Chinese manufacturers.

Sourcing beauty tools directly from Chinese factories can significantly reduce your costs and give you a competitive edge. Here's what you need to know.

### Why Source Directly?

Direct factory sourcing eliminates middlemen, giving you 30-50% cost savings compared to buying from trading companies. You also gain access to custom OEM/ODM services.

### Key Quality Certifications to Look For

Always verify CE, FCC, and ROHS certifications for electronic beauty devices. For cosmetics and daily chemical products, look for GMP and MSDS documentation.
