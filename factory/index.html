Manufacturing Excellence

Our main factory is located in Guangzhou Baiyun — the heart of China's manufacturing hub. Spanning 5,000+ sqm, our facility houses dedicated production lines for each product category: nail supplies, eyelash tools, PMU equipment, beauty devices, and daily chemical products.

  • R&D Center: In-house product development team that tracks global beauty trends and creates new products monthly.
  • Production Lines: 6 dedicated assembly lines with modern equipment for consistent quality at scale.
  • QC Stations: 6-stage quality inspection integrated into every production batch.
  • Warehouse: 2000+ sqm storage capacity for fast order fulfillment.
